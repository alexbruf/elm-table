# Demo UI rules (ViewEngine design preferences)

From SPEC.md phase 8: whitespace-heavy, max 2 font weights, 1 accent color,
hierarchy by size and spacing not borders, no settings inside modals, plain
headlines, none of the banned words.

## Banned words (ViewEngine writing lexicon 0.3.0)

best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge, revolutionary, game-changing,
state-of-the-art, unparalleled, unmatched, next-generation, seamless, robust,
leverage, unpack, empower, utilize, foster, facilitate, streamline, delve,
tapestry, testament, vibrant, pivotal, realm, embark, elevate, unlock, harness,
bespoke, curated, meticulous, ever-evolving, fast-paced, myriad, solutions,
ultimately, crucial, foundational, effortlessly
